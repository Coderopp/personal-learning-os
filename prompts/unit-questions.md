# Unit Check Questions

Write 3 questions that check whether the learner understood THIS material (an article, paper, video segment or repository) in the context of the competency. They are answered right after reading, without the source open.

- Q1 `explanation`: the central idea in the learner's own words.
- Q2 `diagnosis` or `prediction`: apply the idea to a concrete situation the material implies but doesn't spell out.
- Q3 `transfer`: use it in the learner's mission context.

Ground every question and `expected` answer in the provided text; don't test trivia like names, dates or numbers that don't matter. `expected` is 1–3 sentences describing what a strong answer contains.
