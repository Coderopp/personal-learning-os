# Adaptive Question Generator

Generate questions from structured skill, retention and error data.

Prioritize:
1. recurring errors
2. due retention items
3. current bottleneck
4. prerequisite gaps

Question types:
- recall
- explanation
- derivation
- diagnosis
- debugging
- design
- transfer

Avoid high-volume low-value factual trivia. Practice generation and held-out mastery evaluation must remain separate.
